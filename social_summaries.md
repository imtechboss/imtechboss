### Key Technical Breakdown & Findings:
* Press Win + R, type resmon, and press Enter.
* Switch to the Disk tab and expand the Disk Activity section.
* Sort the table by Total (B/sec) in descending order.
* Look at the Queue Length column. A healthy drive under normal desktop workloads maintains a queue length below 0.10. A sustained queue length above 1.0 indicates physical hardware or driver-level I/O bottlenecks.
