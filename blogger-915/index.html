Want To Achieve Your Fitness Goals In 2020, Plan Your Workout Using These 5 Apps

1. JEFIT Workout Tracker, Weight Lifting, Gym Log App
First app on our list is ‘JEFIT Workout Tracker’, this app is so amazing that it had won editor choice award several time. It provides you with free fitness program database to help you stay fit. It can help you with various workout routines like ‘5X5’, ‘3 or 4 days split’ and some advance training regime as well like ‘weightlifting’, ‘powerlifting’ and ‘kettlebell’. It is a very handy app for those who keep track of their training and exercises in journals. JEFIT will help you reach your strength, weight and fat loss, and training goals from beginners to advanced lifters. You can access a huge database of over 1300+ different workouts with instructions given in form of videos and animations.

App Preview
Download -: Google Play Store

2. FitOn - Free Fitness Workouts & Personalized Plans
You don’t have to spend even a penny if you want to get fit or stay fit after you install this app. You can achieve your health and fitness goals with unlimited access to the best workout classes - from exercise styles ranging from cardio to strength training to HIIT, yoga, Pilates, dance, barre and more. All of this training tutorial comes directly from best instructors around the world. The tutorials are short, crisp and to the point. With help of this app you can convert your smartphone into a smart personal gym. The most amazing part of this app is you don’t have to pay a single buck from your pocket. There is no subscription, no in-app purchases no hidden costs.

App Preview
Download -: Google Play Store

3. My Workout Plan
My Workout Plan is your gym buddy that will managing your exercises, creating routines, play your workouts and recording your progress all the way. This app is a great manager app i.e. it manages your workout routine, body weight and more. The app is a boon for those who love to customize everything around them as this app emphasize more on customization and simplicity to provide users with the best experience. Once you install this app you no longer need a gym trainer to make or break your workout routines, you can build your own custom routine keeping in mind your goals, strength and weakness.

App Preview
Download -: Google Play Store

4. Gym Workout Planner
If you are looking for an app that will help you give some extra pump to your muscles then say no more this is the app that is special designed by some professional for that specific purpose. It works as your virtual gym coach and help you start training and transform your body. This app cover each and every aspect that you need to consider while entering the field of bodybuilding. No matter you are beginner or an expert it is going to help you a lot. It is a complete fitness solution for the gym: everything you need to get in shape and stay in shape!

App Preview
Download -: Google Play Store

5. FitNotes
FitNotes is a workout tracker app that is made keeping in mind only two things i.e. simplicity and usability. You can easily use through your daily workout logs just by swiping. It also comes with an inbuilt calendar with help of which you can easily use to year old workouts as well. You can also add an exercise to the workout log and record sets of weight and reps or distance and time. You can also comment and add notes to your routine that can help you get something extra from you regular workout. The most amazing part of this app is it is free to download and ‘Ad- Free’ as well that comes out to be the x-factor while using this app.
