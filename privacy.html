Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Transparency Commitment: At Tech Boss (accessible from https://imtechboss.com), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This document details the types of personal information received and collected by Tech Boss and how it is utilized in strict accordance with global privacy frameworks.

1. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of our site.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require account registration to read our technical journalism, tutorials, or guides. However, you may voluntarily provide personal information when:

  • Contacting our editorial desk via email or contact forms (e.g., your name, email address, message contents).
  • Subscribing to newsletter updates or RSS feeds.

3. Log Files & Web Analytics

Like most standard website servers and edge networks, Tech Boss utilizes log files and privacy-focused telemetry (Cloudflare Web Analytics & Google Analytics 4). The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information and is used solely for analyzing usage trends, administering the site, and improving infrastructure performance.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising (Mandatory Disclosures)

Google is one of our primary third-party vendors. Google uses cookies, specifically the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve advertisements to visitors based on their visit to imtechboss.com and other websites across the internet.

How Visitors Can Opt Out:

  • Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, visitors can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices/ or the Network Advertising Initiative.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Tech Boss, which are sent directly to your browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Other technologies (such as cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons) may also be used by the third-party ad networks to measure the effectiveness of their advertisements and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see.

Please note that Tech Boss has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

5. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to request disclosure of personal data categories collected, request deletion of collected personal data, and demand that a business not sell their personal data. Tech Boss does not sell, trade, or rent personal user data under any circumstance.

6. GDPR Data Protection Rights

Every user in the European Economic Area (EEA) is entitled to the following rights:

  • Right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • Right to rectification: You can request corrections to incomplete or inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure: You can request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of young children online is especially important. Tech Boss does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided this information on our website, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such records from our databases.
