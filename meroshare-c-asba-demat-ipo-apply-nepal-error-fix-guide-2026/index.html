Quick Answer: To apply for IPOs, FPOs, or Right Shares in Nepal via Meroshare (meroshare.cdsc.com.np), you need three linked credentials: an active Demat BOID (16 digits) from a Depository Participant (DP), a Meroshare login, and a C-ASBA CRN number issued by your commercial bank holding sufficient NPR balance.

1. How Demat, Meroshare & C-ASBA Work Together in Nepal

Many first-time Nepali investors open a Demat account and assume they can apply for IPOs immediately—only to get stuck on the CRN Number screen inside Meroshare. Understanding each layer clears up every error:

ComponentWho Issues It?Annual Fee (NPR)Purpose
Demat Account (16-digit BOID)Merchant Bank / Broker DPRs. 100 / yearHolds your allotted shares electronically in CDSC
Meroshare Portal / AppCDSC (via your DP)Rs. 50 / yearOnline dashboard to apply for IPOs, EDIS & check portfolio
C-ASBA & CRN NumberYour Commercial BankRs. 0 – Rs. 5 per IPOBlocks your IPO amount (e.g. Rs. 1,000 for 10 kitta) inside your bank

2. Step-by-Step: Applying for an IPO via Meroshare

  1. Verify Account Validity First: Log into meroshare.cdsc.com.np or the official MeroShare app. Check My Profile to confirm neither your Demat nor Meroshare subscription has expired.
  2. Navigate to My ASBA → Apply for Issue: Select the open IPO/debenture/mutual fund issue and click Apply.
  3. Select Bank, Applied Kitta & CRN: Choose your C-ASBA-linked bank account, enter the kitta quantity (10 kitta = Rs. 1,000 for standard Rs. 100 par value IPOs), type your CRN number, and check the declaration box.
  4. Confirm With 4-Digit Transaction PIN: Enter your Meroshare Transaction PIN. Your status changes from Unverified to Verified once your bank places the ASBA hold on your funds.
  5. Check Allotment Result: After issue closing and allotment, check Application Report inside My ASBA or iporesult.cdsc.com.np using your 16-digit BOID.

3. Fixing the 4 Most Common Meroshare & C-ASBA Errors

  • "Account Expired / Restricted": Pay the Rs. 150 combined Demat + Meroshare renewal fee inside My Profile → Renew Account via ConnectIPS, eSewa, or Khalti. Note that if Demat stays unpaid across multiple years, late/freeze penalties can require visiting your DP counter.
  • "Insufficient Fund" Despite Balance Showing: Your bank may have a minimum savings balance requirement (e.g., Rs. 500 or Rs. 1,000) that cannot be blocked by ASBA—keep at least Rs. 1,500 above your minimum balance before applying.
  • EDIS / WACC Error After Selling Secondary Shares: Always complete My Purchase Source (WACC) and My EDIS (Transfer Shares) before 5:00 PM on T+1 settlement day after selling shares through TMS to avoid the 20% close-out penalty.
  • Planning Long-Term SIPs or Loans? Compare mutual fund SIP compounding against bank loan EMIs using our Nepal Bank Loan EMI & SIP Calculator, and estimate your annual tax deductions with our Nepal Salary Tax & SSF Calculator.

5. Applying for Family Member IPOs & Avoiding Multiple-Demat Rejection

Under SEBON and CDSC rules, a single citizen can hold up to two Demat accounts, but you are strictly limited to one IPO application per person per issue. Submitting two applications under the same Citizenship/BOID holder automatically invalidates all applications from that individual during C-ASBA verification.

If you manage separate Meroshare logins for parents or siblings, ensure each family member links their own C-ASBA CRN from their personal bank account rather than applying through a single third-party account unless your bank explicitly supports minor/joint guardian CRN mapping. Always verify your National ID (NID) and PAN details match your broker KYC so dividend payouts (cash dividends via IPS) credit smoothly without bank holdbacks. Keeping your Demat and Meroshare renewal dates tracked alongside your TMS broker account prevents missed IPO windows and unexpected settlement penalties during busy primary market weeks.

Frequently Asked Questions & Technical Clarifications

Where do I get my C-ASBA CRN number for Meroshare IPO applications? ▼

You receive your CRN (C-ASBA Registration Number) directly from your commercial bank branch or mobile banking app (under C-ASBA registration) after linking your 16-digit BOID Demat number.

Why does Meroshare show 'Your Demat or Meroshare account is expired' during IPO apply? ▼

CDSC charges an annual renewal fee (Rs. 100 for Demat and Rs. 50 for Meroshare). You can pay both directly inside Meroshare -> My Profile -> Renew Account using eSewa, Khalti, ConnectIPS, or IME Pay.

How do I fix 'Invalid Transaction PIN' or locked Meroshare login? ▼

Reset your login password via 'Forgot Password' using your DP, Username, and registered Email, or contact your Capital / Merchant Bank DP desk to unlock your account and reset your 4-digit Transaction PIN.
