About Tech Boss

Authoritative, fast-paced technical journalism covering Frontier AI, PC hardware engineering, and digital utility tools.

Our Mission

Founded by technology developer and editor Binod Bhatt, Tech Boss (imtechboss.com) was established with a singular objective: cut through corporate marketing spin to deliver accurate, real-world engineering insights, in-depth hardware benchmarks, and practical guides for engineers, creators, and technology enthusiasts worldwide.

What We Cover

🤖 Frontier AI & LLMs

Deep architectural breakdowns of model training, GPU clusters, local inferencing (Ollama, LM Studio), and AI ethics.

⚡ Cloud & Datacenter Hardware

Silicon advancements from Nvidia, AMD, Intel, and Apple, exploring thermal density, liquid cooling, and wafer scale.

🎮 PC Gaming & Performance

Frame-time pacing, GPU driver optimizations, Windows 11 system tweaks, and gaming title deep dives.

🛠️ Software Utilities & Security

Cybersecurity hardening, zero-log VPN analysis, open-source utilities, and consumer privacy protection.

Editorial Integrity & Standards (E-E-A-T)

Every guide, analysis, and news report published on Tech Boss adheres to strict verification principles:

  • Zero Synthetic Jargon: We avoid AI clichés and regurgitated press releases. We ground our analyses in technical datasheets and real hardware benchmarks.
  • Independent Testing: Software recommendations, VPN speeds, and Windows optimization scripts are independently verified before being published.
  • Transparent Corrections: In the fast-moving tech sector, if an error or updated vendor firmware changes a conclusion, we immediately post an update log at the top of the story.
