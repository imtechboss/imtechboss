Quick Answer: To obtain an official Nepal Police Clearance Report (OPCR) in 2026, register an account at opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np, upload clear scans of your citizenship certificate, e-Passport bio page, and a white-background passport photo, then download your QR-verifiable digital certificate in 3–5 working days for free.

1. When You Need an Official Police Clearance Report (OPCR)

A Police Clearance Certificate (PCC / OPCR) issued by Nepal Police Headquarters confirms whether an applicant has any criminal record in Nepal. Foreign embassies, universities, immigration departments, and international employers require a recent OPCR (typically issued within the last 6 months) for student visas alongside your NOC certificate, skilled migration (PR), work permits, and foreign residency renewals.

3. Step-by-Step: Applying on opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np

  1. Create Your OPCR Account: Open opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np, click Register, select Nepali (or Foreigner), and enter your active email address and mobile number.
  2. Complete Identity & Family Details: Fill in your full name exactly as printed on your e-Passport, citizenship number, issuing district, and both permanent and current residential addresses (province, district, municipality, and ward number).
  3. Upload Clean Documents & Live Photo: Upload a front-facing color photo on a plain white background (no glasses, no shadows) plus sharp scans of both sides of your citizenship certificate.
  4. Review & Submit Application: Verify every spelling twice—once submitted, you cannot edit details until Nepal Police marks the form for correction.
  5. Track Status & Download Verified PDF: Log back into your dashboard to check status: Submitted → Under Verification → Verified → Report Ready. Download the digitally signed PDF containing the official QR code.

4. Fixing "Re-Apply / Incomplete" Rejections & MoFA Consular Attestation

  • Mismatch Between Citizenship & Passport Spelling: If your citizenship lists an old municipality/VDC name while your passport uses the restructured local level, enter the current municipality in the address dropdown and clearly upload any migration (Basai Sarai) or marriage certificate under supporting documents.
  • MoFA Consular Attestation (Tripureshwor): Embassies in the Gulf, Europe, and several study destinations require your OPCR PDF to be attested by the Department of Consular Services (nepalconsular.gov.np). Upload your approved OPCR reference number directly on the Consular online portal, pay the Rs. 500 attestation fee via ConnectIPS or eSewa, and download the attested e-stamp without waiting in line.

5. How Embassies Verify Nepal OPCR QR Codes & Validity Period

Once your digital Police Clearance Report is issued, print it in high-resolution color or upload the original unmodified PDF directly to your embassy portal. Every OPCR certificate generated by Nepal Police Headquarters carries a cryptographically signed QR code and a unique reference number that foreign visa officers scan directly against opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np/verify to confirm authenticity.

Most destination countries—including Australia, Canada, the UK, Japan, and Schengen states—accept a Nepal OPCR for 6 months from the date of issue. Never alter, rename metadata, or re-scan a clean digital PDF if the embassy accepts electronic uploads; submit the exact PDF downloaded from your OPCR dashboard. Frequently Asked Questions & Technical Clarifications

How much does a Nepal Police Clearance Report (OPCR) cost online? ▼

Applying for an Online Police Clearance Report through opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np is completely free (Rs. 0). You only pay a separate revenue fee if you later need Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) consular attestation.

How many days does Nepal Police take to issue an OPCR certificate? ▼

Standard online verification takes 3 to 5 working days if your citizenship, passport, and local ward details match cleanly. Applications with incomplete address details or manual district checks can take 7 to 10 working days.

Can Nepali citizens living abroad apply for an OPCR online? ▼

Yes. Both applicants inside Nepal and Nepali citizens residing abroad can register on opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np, upload passport bio/arrival pages, and download the QR-verified PDF certificate online.
