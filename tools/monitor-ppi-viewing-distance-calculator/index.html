How to Calculate Monitor PPI, Retina Distance & DisplayPort 2.1 Headroom

🖥️ Pixel Density (PPI) & Visual Acuity Limit

  • 20/20 Acuity Formula: Human vision resolves approximately 1 arcminute (1/60°). The threshold distance where pixels blend into continuous imagery equals 3438 / PPI inches.
  • The 110 PPI Desktop Baseline: Standard 24" 1080p (91.8 PPI) and 27" 1440p (108.8 PPI) offer ideal UI scaling without requiring OS fractional scaling interpolation.

⚡ DisplayPort 2.1 vs. HDMI 2.1 vs. VESA DSC

  • DisplayPort 1.4a Ceiling: 25.92 Gbps data cap handles 1440p 240Hz uncompressed, but requires VESA DSC 3:1 for 4K 144Hz+ or 4K 240Hz.
  • DisplayPort 2.1 UHBR20: Delivers 77.37 Gbps uncompressed bandwidth, driving 4K 240Hz 10-bit HDR natively with zero compression latency or GPU display engine sleep quirks.
