How to Verify Authentic Laptops in Nepal (Putalisadak, New Road & Online)

Laptop prices in Nepal fluctuate based on official authorized imports versus grey-market channels. Before paying Rs. 80,000 to Rs. 2,50,000, always verify three things in-store: check the serial number directly on Lenovo/Asus/Acer/Apple's official warranty portal, inspect SSD power-on hours using CrystalDiskInfo (a brand-new laptop should show under 5–10 hours), and confirm whether RAM is upgradeable or soldered.
