Nepal Salary Tax, SSF & 5% IT Export Calculator (FY 2082/83)

⚡ Nepal Tax Slabs & 5% IT Export Rule Salaried individuals in Nepal pay 1% Social Security Tax (SST) on the first Rs. 5,00,000 (Single) or Rs. 6,00,000 (Married), followed by progressive 10%, 20%, 30%, 36%, and 39% slabs. Registered SSF contributors are exempt from the 1% SST on the base slab. Foreign software/IT export remittances remain eligible for 5% final withholding tax.

How Nepal Salary Tax, SSF & 5% IT Export TDS Are Calculated

In Nepal, you must hold a Personal PAN number (easily generated via Nagarik App) for employer TDS reconciliation or 5% IT Export tax bank certificates.
