2026 GPU & Hardware Matcher Quiz

⚡ GPU Buying Strategy in 2026: Selecting the right GPU depends primarily on memory bandwidth, VRAM buffer capacity, and target display resolution. For pure 1080p gaming, 8GB to 12GB is sufficient. For 1440p gaming and local AI inference (DeepSeek/Ollama), 16GB is the mandatory baseline. For 4K gaming and 70B parameter models, 24GB is essential.

🎮 Gaming Tier Breakdown

  • Budget ($250 – $350): RTX 4060 / RX 7600 XT — High-refresh 1080p esports and AAA medium settings.
