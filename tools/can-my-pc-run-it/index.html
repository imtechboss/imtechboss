PC Gaming Hardware Requirements & Minimum Specs Explained

🕹️ Modern System Bottlenecks

  • 8GB VRAM Cliff: Games like Cyberpunk 2077, Hogwarts Legacy, and GTA 6 experience severe stuttering on 8GB cards at 1440p due to VRAM overflow into system RAM.
  • DirectStorage SSD Requirement: Running modern open-world games on mechanical hard drives (HDDs) causes massive texture pop-in and audio sync failures.

🚀 Upscaling & Frame Generation

  • DLSS 3.5 / FSR 3.1: Renders at 67% internal resolution while reconstructing sharp 1440p/4K visuals, boosting frame rates by up to 60%.
  • Reflex & Anti-Lag: Crucial low-latency features that reduce frame buffering lag when GPU utilization hits 99%.
