Nepal Land Area Converter (Ropani-Aana & Bigha-Kattha)

⚡ Standard Nepal Survey Conversion 1 Ropani equals 16 Aana (5,476 sq. ft / 508.74 sq. m) and 1 Aana equals 4 Paisa (342.25 sq. ft). In the Terai system, 1 Bigha equals 20 Kattha (72,900 sq. ft) and 1 Kattha equals 20 Dhur (3,645 sq. ft). Enter any measurement below for instant conversion and NPR valuation.

Understanding Ropani-Aana vs Bigha-Kattha & Mero Kitta Sq. Meter Values

On official Nepali Lalpurja and Mero Kitta records, land area is often stored in square meters (sq. m) or R-A-P-D / B-K-D notation. One Ropani equals 508.74 sq. m (5,476 sq. ft), while one Kattha equals 338.63 sq. m (3,645 sq. ft).

For multi-parcel subdivision (Kitta Kaat) and irregular plot area geometry, explore Land Solution Nepal Cadastral Software.
