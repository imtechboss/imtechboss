NEA Electricity Bill & Inverter Backup Calculator Nepal

⚡ Official NEA Domestic Slab Tariff Estimator Enter your monthly kWh units and meter ampere rating (5A, 15A, 30A, or 60A) to calculate your exact NEA minimum service charge, progressive energy charge per slab, 2% early payment rebate, and optional home inverter backup duration.

Saving Money on NEA Domestic Bills & 2% Early Payment Rebate

NEA gives a 2% rebate when you settle your bill within 7 days of meter reading using digital wallets or ConnectIPS. Households using induction stoves and geysers should upgrade from 5A to a 15A/30A meter to prevent MCB tripping.
