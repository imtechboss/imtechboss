⚡ ATX 3.1 vs. Legacy ATX 2.4 Standards

  • Transient Excursion Handling: ATX 3.1 mandates that the power supply sustain 200% of its rated wattage for 100 microseconds without tripping internal protection circuits.
  • 12V-2x6 Connector Safety: Replaces fragile 12VHPWR headers with recessed 4-pin sideband contacts that prevent terminal pin melting even if seated imperfectly.

80 PLUS Efficiency Curves

  • The 50% Load Sweet Spot: Power supplies achieve maximum electrical conversion efficiency (80 Plus Gold 90%+, Platinum 92%+) when operating between 45% and 60% of total capacity.
  • Thermal & Acoustic Benefit: Running a 1000W PSU at 500W load allows zero-RPM fan modes to remain active, keeping your gaming PC virtually silent under gaming workloads.
