NVMe SSD PCIe Gen 3 vs Gen 4 vs Gen 5 Speed Calculator

⚡ Real M.2 Slot Throughput Ceiling NVMe drives negotiate down to the lower PCIe generation and lane width of either the drive or motherboard M.2 slot. Check your real effective ceiling before upgrading.

Understanding Real-World NVMe Speed vs. Marketing Box Numbers

Sequential throughput (e.g., 7,400 MB/s on PCIe Gen 4 or 14,500 MB/s on PCIe Gen 5) only matters when copying huge video files or loading 20GB+ local LLM GGUF weights from disk into VRAM. For Windows boot times and application launches, 4K random read latency (QD1) matters far more than raw sequential bandwidth.
