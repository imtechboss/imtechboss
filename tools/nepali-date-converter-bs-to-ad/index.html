Nepali Date Converter (BS ↔ AD) & Exact Age Calculator

⚡ Accurate BS ↔ AD Date Conversion for Official Forms Convert dates between Bikram Sambat (BS 2000–2090) and Gregorian (AD 1943–2033) directly in your browser. Verify exact birthdates and issue dates for Nepal e-Passport, NID, PAN, and Loksewa applications, and compute exact age in years, months, and days.

Why Accurate BS to AD Conversion Matters on Nepali Portals

Government systems like DoNIDCR (NID), Department of Passports, and IRD PAN validate your Bikram Sambat birthdate against its exact Gregorian equivalent. A one-day discrepancy can trigger CIMS mismatch errors.
