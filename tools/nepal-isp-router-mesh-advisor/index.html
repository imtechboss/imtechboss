Why Fiber Speed Drops Upstairs in Nepali Concrete Homes

Even on a 200–300 Mbps fiber plan from WorldLink, Vianet, or CGNet, an ISP-provided single ONT router struggles to penetrate reinforced concrete slabs (RCC) and brick walls. Cheap wireless Wi-Fi repeaters amplify weak signal while halving bandwidth and doubling ping jitter.

The cleanest fix in Nepal is running one affordable Cat6 cable from the main ONT LAN port to a secondary Wi-Fi 6 router placed upstairs in Access Point (AP) mode. See our full WorldLink, Vianet & CGNet Router Optimization Guide.
