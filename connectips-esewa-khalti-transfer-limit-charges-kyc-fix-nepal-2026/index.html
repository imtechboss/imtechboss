2. How to Link & Self-Verify Your Bank Account in ConnectIPS

  1. Register on ConnectIPS App/Web: Sign up using the exact mobile number and email registered with your Nepali commercial bank.
  2. Add Bank Account: Go to Bank Account → Link Account, pick your bank, and enter your exact account number and account name.
  3. Self-Verify Without Visiting Branch: Tap Self-Verify Account. ConnectIPS sends two micro-deposits (for example, Rs. 0.14 and Rs. 0.27) to your bank account; enter those exact amounts from your mobile banking SMS/statement to activate instant transfers (note: self-verified limits are lower than branch-verified limits until you submit the 1-page signed form at your bank).

3. Fixing Wallet KYC Rejections, Pending Transfers & OTP Failures

  • eSewa / Khalti KYC Rejected ("Document Unclear"): Take your citizenship photo in daylight on a dark table with all four corners visible, and ensure your English name matches your citizenship and Personal PAN exactly. If the app rejects large camera files, shrink them cleanly under 200KB via our Nepal Govt Form Photo & Document Compressor.
  • Amount Debited From Bank But Transaction Failed: Never repeat the payment immediately while status shows Pending. Copy the NCHL / Fonepay Reference ID, wait 30 minutes for auto-reversal, and if not refunded, email your bank's card/digital support with the screenshot for standard T+1 working day settlement reversal.
  • Planning Loan EMI or Monthly Savings? If you use ConnectIPS or mobile banking for monthly bank installments or SIP auto-debits, estimate your exact monthly outflow with our Nepal Bank Loan EMI & SIP Calculator.

4. Smart Security Rules & Fee-Saving Habits for Nepali Digital Payments

As digital transaction volume grows across Nepal, phishing SMS links and fake customer-care Facebook pages targeting eSewa, Khalti, and mobile banking users have increased sharply. Never share your OTP, MPIN, or ConnectIPS transaction password with anyone claiming to be bank support or a buyer on marketplace listings—receiving money into your wallet or bank account never requires entering an OTP or scanning a payment QR.

To minimize monthly fees, link your primary salary or freelance bank account directly to ConnectIPS for rent, tuition, IRD tax vouchers, and broker TMS settlements (Rs. 2–Rs. 8 per transfer), while keeping a smaller working balance in eSewa or Khalti for daily QR coffee, grocery, and utility scans. Frequently Asked Questions & Technical Clarifications

What is the maximum daily bank transfer limit on ConnectIPS in Nepal? ▼

Under Nepal Rastra Bank payment guidelines, verified ConnectIPS users can transfer up to Rs. 2,00,000 per transaction and Rs. 20,00,000 per day on web (and up to Rs. 2,00,000 per day on mobile app, depending on individual bank caps).

How much does ConnectIPS charge for interbank fund transfers? ▼

ConnectIPS charges Rs. 2 for transfers up to Rs. 500, Rs. 4 for Rs. 501 to Rs. 5,000, and Rs. 8 for transfers above Rs. 5,000 (standard retail slab), making it cheaper than Fonepay Rs. 10–11 flat charges for small amounts.

What should I do if money is deducted from my bank but not loaded into eSewa or Khalti? ▼

Save the transaction reference ID and wait 15–30 minutes for automated reversal. If it does not reverse automatically, submit the reference ID to your bank's digital support or wallet helpdesk for NRB-mandated T+1 settlement reconciliation.
